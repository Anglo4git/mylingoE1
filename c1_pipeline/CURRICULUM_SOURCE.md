## Speaking — Interaction and Production
Learners will be able to:
- Express ideas fluently and spontaneously without much obvious searching
  for expressions.
- Use language flexibly and effectively for social, academic, and
  professional purposes.
- Produce clear, well-structured, detailed text on complex subjects,
  showing controlled use of organisational patterns, connectors, and
  cohesive devices.

## Listening
Learners will be able to:
- Understand extended speech even when not clearly structured and when
  relationships are only implied, not signalled explicitly.
- Understand television programmes and films without too much effort.

## Reading
Learners will be able to:
- Understand long, complex factual and literary texts, appreciating
  distinctions of style.
- Understand specialised articles and longer technical instructions, even
  when they do not relate to their own field.

## Writing
Learners will be able to:
- Write clear, well-structured text on complex subjects, underlining the
  relevant salient issues.
- Write in a style appropriate to the reader, selecting an effective
  structure and register for the context.
- Summarise and evaluate information and arguments from a number of
  sources.
