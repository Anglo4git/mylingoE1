## Speaking — Interaction and Production
Learners will be able to:
- Deal with most situations when travelling in an area where English is spoken.
- Talk about family, hobbies, work, travel, and current events.
- Talk about experiences and events, dreams, hopes, and ambitions.
- Give opinions and explanations.
- Tell the story of a book or film and describe their reactions.

## Listening
Learners will be able to:
- Understand the main points of clear speech on work and school.
- Understand the main points of radio or TV programmes on current affairs
  or familiar topics, when the speaker speaks slowly and clearly.

## Reading
Learners will be able to:
- Understand simple everyday language and basic vocabulary.
- Understand descriptions of events, feelings, and wishes in personal letters.

## Writing
Learners will be able to:
- Write simply about familiar topics.
- Write personal letters describing experiences and impressions.
